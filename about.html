Dennis Martin, M.Ed., B.Ed., OCT — Toronto-based senior learning strategist, leadership development professional, and organizational transformation practitioner.

My story

My career began in classrooms — practice teaching with the Near North and Toronto District School Boards, and supporting fellow teacher candidates as a Student Technology Assistant at Nipissing University. I hold Ontario College of Teachers certification (OCT #632749) with teachables in Computer Studies and History, and that evidence-based pedagogy still anchors how I design for adults.

In North Bay, as a Corporate Trainer at HGS Canada, I trained 23 month-long cohorts for Bell Mobility, Virgin Mobile, Solo and PC Mobile — earning the department’s lowest attrition and highest satisfaction, and an Employee Achievement Award for metacognitive learning activities that were later adopted across other departments nationally.

Moving to Toronto and The 519, I shifted to community and institutional education: redesigning curriculum and facilitator guides, building assessment tools on the CTDP competency framework, and co-facilitating more than 100 sessions — including PrideHouseTO, Region of Peel, and Ontario Public Service LGBTQ2S+ inclusion programs.

At Circle of Care, Sinai Health System (2017–2022), I progressed from Curriculum Developer to L&D Specialist to Manager, Organizational Learning & People Development. There I implemented Dayforce across HR modules, redesigned onboarding into a digital journey, created the PSW Portal for union-represented field staff, and designed the organization-wide LEADS Leadership Development Program as a certified LEADS Facilitator/Integrator. As a manager, I led a team of three and led the digital reach initiative that gave PSWs digital pay, LMS and benefits access for the first time in the organization’s history.

Since August 2022 at Infrastructure Ontario, I’ve worked at enterprise scale: leading the SAP SuccessFactors LMS implementation to go-live, then the migration to Dayforce Learning; building the Career Development Hub and Leadership Development Hub in SharePoint; conducting 50+ leadership assessments; building 40+ Power BI dashboards; and developing AI agents and development-planning tools as a member of the AI Task Force. I also lead the Rainbow Alliance ERG.

Leadership philosophy — in full

Developing people is not a function of organizational strategy — it is organizational strategy. I lead with a learner-centred orientation, beginning with curiosity rather than assumption: seeking to understand context, need, and the systemic forces that shape how people show up at work.

The LEADS in a Caring Environment framework is a genuine lens for my work, not just a credential: Leading self, Engaging others, Achieving results, Developing coalitions, and Systems transformation are simultaneous, interdependent disciplines. My Co-Active coaching orientation means I work alongside leaders and teams rather than positioning myself as the expert above them.

Equity, diversity and inclusion are non-negotiable foundations. Every solution I design is interrogated for whose knowledge is centred, whose experience is assumed as default, and who may be excluded by choices that appear neutral. A continuous improvement culture — one that honours both data-informed accountability and humanity — is, in my view, the most durable advantage any organization can build.

How I work

  • Start with the system: structures, policies and informal norms enable or constrain people — I design interventions at that level, not just the individual course.
  • Make technology accessible: recognized repeatedly for translating complex systems (LMS, Dayforce, Power BI) into tools cross-functional partners can actually use.
  • Measure what matters: Kirkpatrick-informed evaluation, learning metrics baselines, and talent analytics that inform procurement and succession decisions.
  • Facilitate for safety: health, safety and psychological-safety moments are a standard part of every session I run.
