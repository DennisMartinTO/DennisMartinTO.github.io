A short version of a longer story

I started as a certified teacher in Ontario — Computer Studies and History — and a corporate trainer in North Bay. That classroom foundation never left my practice: clear outcomes, differentiated design, and a psychologically safe room.

From The 519’s community education work, to Circle of Care / Sinai Health where I built LEADS leadership programming and brought digital access to frontline PSWs for the first time, to Infrastructure Ontario where I lead enterprise learning systems and leadership development — the thread is the same: design at the systems level, not just the course level.
