SharePoint · Career Development

Career Development Hub (“Blueprint”)

Infrastructure Ontario, 2025

I designed and built the Career Development Hub entirely from the ground up in SharePoint — a centralized platform integrating career development, learning and performance resources, with modern navigation, Quick Links, a News section with Power Automate-enabled sorting, a Featured Courses component, Mobility Moments (employee career storytelling), and a Development Opportunities section built with partners including WXN and Pride at Work Canada. I also led creation of a companion Leadership Development Hub, scanning external organizations including Metrolinx and recommending SharePoint over Dayforce on functionality, with a working demo for stakeholder approval.

What it delivered

  • Direct commendation from Jenn Cruz, VP People & Culture (June 2025)
  • A single, beautifully designed home for career, learning and performance at IO
  • Reusable Blueprint pattern for future hubs

Digital Inclusion · Dayforce

Digital Reach for Frontline PSWs + PSW Portal

Circle of Care, Sinai Health System

I led the complete digital reach initiative that launched digital pay stubs, LMS access and benefits enrolment to all PSW union staff via Dayforce — frontline, home-based workers accessing their professional records digitally for the first time in the organization’s history — coordinating People & Culture, Payroll, IT, Communications and Finance. Earlier I built a PSW Portal website as a content management system for policies, resources and communications, co-created the ‘Inside the Circle’ weekly newsletter (described by staff and managers as a ‘game changer’), implemented WalkMe for technology onboarding, and redesigned orientation into a digital onboarding journey.

What it delivered

  • Digital equity for a unionized, community-based frontline workforce
  • Manager rating: Exceptional (5/5) — ‘went above and beyond in becoming the SME’
  • A new, direct communication channel to PSWs
